Why blocking your wallet is unlawful

Unless there is a specific court order, private companies cannot seize your business assets indefinitely. We leverage three key legal frameworks to protect you.

gavel

Consumer Protection Act 2019

Specific clauses protect sellers against unfair trade practices. Withholding legitimate business funds without a tangible dispute resolution mechanism is a direct violation.

account_balance

RBI Payment Guidelines

Intermediaries are not banks. RBI norms strictly regulate how and when payment aggregators can freeze settlements (Nodal Account guidelines).

warning

Arbitrary Action

Legal precedence requires prior notice and a valid, specific reason before any asset freezing can occur. Silent blocking is legally indefensible.
