Marked Attempted But No One Came?

Immediate steps to freeze the RTO process and create an undeniable paper trail.

Correct Action Flow

Check your CCTV or security logs for the exact time the delivery was marked "failed". Save a snapshot if possible.

videocam CCTV

Must be done within 2 hours of the status update. Use the specific dispute option for "Fake Attempt".

Screenshots of your call history prove the agent never called you. This is vital evidence for the claim.

Do NOT share OTP remotely

Delivery agents may call asking for OTP to "reschedule". Sharing this marks the item as delivered or rejected in the system, voiding your insurance.
