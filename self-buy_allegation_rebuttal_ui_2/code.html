Why Platforms Use Self-Buy Allegations to Silence You

Platforms often flag entire accounts for 'Self-Buy' to freeze payouts on all shipments simultaneously. This tactic forces sellers into silence due to cash flow panic. This tool isolates specific orders to legally prove legitimacy and unfreeze your capital.
